# People and Pronouns

## Personal Pronouns
"I" and "me" are "Ako" in Tagalog and "Ako" in Bisaya. "You" is "Ikaw" in both Tagalog and Bisaya. "He" and "she" are both "Siya" in Tagalog and "Siya" in Bisaya. "We" can be "Kami" or "Tayo" in Tagalog and "Kami" or "Kita" in Bisaya. "They" is "Sila" in both languages.

"My" is "Aking" in Tagalog and "Akong" in Bisaya. "Your" is "Iyong" in Tagalog and "Imong" in Bisaya. "Our" can be "Ating" or "Aming" in Tagalog and "Atong" or "Among" in Bisaya. "Their" is "Kanilang" in Tagalog and "Ilang" in Bisaya.

## Family and People
"Friend" is "Kaibigan" in Tagalog and "Higala" in Bisaya. "Family" is "Pamilya" in both languages. "Mother" is "Nanay" or "Ina" in Tagalog and "Mama" or "Inahan" in Bisaya. "Father" is "Tatay" or "Ama" in Tagalog and "Papa" or "Amahan" in Bisaya.

"Brother" is "Kapatid na lalaki" in Tagalog and "Igsoon nga lalaki" in Bisaya. "Sister" is "Kapatid na babae" in Tagalog and "Igsoon nga babaye" in Bisaya. "Child" is "Bata" in both languages. "Man" is "Lalaki" in both languages. "Woman" is "Babae" in Tagalog and "Babaye" in Bisaya. "Person" is "Tao" in Tagalog and "Tawo" in Bisaya.
