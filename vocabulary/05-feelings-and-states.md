# Feelings and States

## Common Feelings
"Happy" is "Masaya" in Tagalog and "Malipayon" in Bisaya. "Sad" is "Malungkot" in Tagalog and "Masulob-on" in Bisaya. "Angry" is "Galit" in Tagalog and "Nasuko" in Bisaya. "Afraid" is "Takot" in Tagalog and "Nahadlok" in Bisaya.

"Excited" is "Nasasabik" in Tagalog and "Excited" in Bisaya. "Tired" is "Pagod" in Tagalog and "Kapoy" in Bisaya. "Hungry" is "Gutom" in Tagalog and "Gigutom" in Bisaya. "Thirsty" is "Uhaw" in Tagalog and "Giuhaw" in Bisaya.

## Conditions
"Sleepy" is "Inaantok" in Tagalog and "Katulgon" in Bisaya. "Sick" is "May sakit" in Tagalog and "Masakiton" in Bisaya. "Fine" is "Mabuti" or "Ayos" in Tagalog and "Maayo" or "Okay" in Bisaya. "Ready" is "Handa" in Tagalog and "Andam" in Bisaya.

"Busy" is "Abala" in Tagalog and "Busy" in Bisaya. "Confused" is "Nalilito" in Tagalog and "Naglibog" in Bisaya. "Worried" is "Nag-aalala" in Tagalog and "Nabalaka" in Bisaya. "Bored" is "Nababagot" in Tagalog and "Nainip" in Bisaya.
