# Greetings and Basic Phrases

## Greetings
English translation requests about greetings should use these translations. "Hello" and "Hi" translate to "Kumusta" in Tagalog and "Kumusta" in Bisaya (Cebuano). "Good morning" is "Magandang umaga" in Tagalog and "Maayong buntag" in Bisaya. "Good afternoon" is "Magandang hapon" in Tagalog and "Maayong hapon" in Bisaya. "Good evening" is "Magandang gabi" in Tagalog and "Maayong gabii" in Bisaya.

"Goodbye" is "Paalam" in Tagalog and "Babay" in Bisaya. "Good night" is "Magandang gabi" in Tagalog and "Maayong gabii" in Bisaya. "See you" is "Kita tayo" in Tagalog and "Kita ta" in Bisaya.

## Thanks and Politeness
"Thank you" is "Salamat" in both Tagalog and Bisaya. "Thanks a lot" is "Maraming salamat" in Tagalog and "Daghang salamat" in Bisaya. "You're welcome" is "Walang anuman" in Tagalog and "Walay sapayan" in Bisaya. "Please" is commonly "Pakiusap" in Tagalog and "Palihog" in Bisaya. "Sorry" is "Pasensya na" in Tagalog and "Pasayloa ko" in Bisaya. "Excuse me" is "Makikiraan" in Tagalog and "Agian ko" in Bisaya.

## Common Responses
"Yes" is "Oo" in both languages. "No" is "Hindi" in Tagalog and "Dili" in Bisaya. "Maybe" is "Siguro" in Tagalog and "Tingali" in Bisaya. "Of course" is "Siyempre" in both languages. "Really?" is "Talaga?" in Tagalog and "Tinood?" in Bisaya. "That's right" is "Tama iyan" in Tagalog and "Mao kana" in Bisaya.
