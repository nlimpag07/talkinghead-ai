# Questions and Conversation

## Question Words
"What?" is "Ano?" in Tagalog and "Unsa?" in Bisaya. "Who?" is "Sino?" in Tagalog and "Kinsa?" in Bisaya. "Where?" is "Saan?" in Tagalog and "Asa?" in Bisaya. "When?" is "Kailan?" in Tagalog and "Kanus-a?" in Bisaya. "Why?" is "Bakit?" in Tagalog and "Ngano?" in Bisaya. "How?" is "Paano?" in Tagalog and "Giunsa?" in Bisaya.

"How much?" is "Magkano?" in Tagalog and "Tagpila?" in Bisaya. "How many?" is "Ilan?" in Tagalog and "Pila?" in Bisaya. "Which?" is "Alin?" in Tagalog and "Asa?" in Bisaya.

## Everyday Questions
"What is this?" is "Ano ito?" in Tagalog and "Unsa ni?" in Bisaya. "What is that?" is "Ano iyon?" in Tagalog and "Unsa kana?" in Bisaya. "Who is that?" is "Sino iyon?" in Tagalog and "Kinsa kana?" in Bisaya.

"Where are you?" is "Nasaan ka?" in Tagalog and "Asa ka?" in Bisaya. "Where are you going?" is "Saan ka pupunta?" in Tagalog and "Asa ka paingon?" in Bisaya. "What are you doing?" is "Ano ang ginagawa mo?" in Tagalog and "Unsa imong gibuhat?" in Bisaya. "What happened?" is "Ano ang nangyari?" in Tagalog and "Unsa ang nahitabo?" in Bisaya.

"Are you okay?" is "Okay ka lang ba?" in Tagalog and "Okay ra ka?" in Bisaya. "Do you understand?" is "Naiintindihan mo ba?" in Tagalog and "Kasabot ka?" in Bisaya. "Do you know?" is "Alam mo ba?" in Tagalog and "Nahibalo ka?" in Bisaya.
