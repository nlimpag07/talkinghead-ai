# Location and Direction

## Directions
"Left" is "Kaliwa" in Tagalog and "Wala" in Bisaya. "Right" is "Kanan" in Tagalog and "Tuo" in Bisaya. "Straight" is "Diretso" in Tagalog and "Tul-id" in Bisaya.

"Near" is "Malapit" in Tagalog and "Duol" in Bisaya. "Far" is "Malayo" in Tagalog and "Layo" in Bisaya. "Inside" is "Loob" in Tagalog and "Sulod" in Bisaya. "Outside" is "Labas" in Tagalog and "Gawas" in Bisaya.

## Common Location Questions
"Where is it?" is "Nasaan ito?" in Tagalog and "Asa kini?" in Bisaya. "Where do you live?" is "Saan ka nakatira?" in Tagalog and "Asa ka nagpuyo?" in Bisaya. "Where are you from?" is "Taga-saan ka?" in Tagalog and "Taga-asa ka?" in Bisaya.

"How much is this?" is "Magkano ito?" in Tagalog and "Tagpila ni?" in Bisaya. "What time is it?" is "Anong oras na?" in Tagalog and "Unsa na orasa?" in Bisaya.
