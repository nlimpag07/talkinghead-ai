# Descriptions and Qualities

## Size and Physical Qualities
"Big" is "Malaki" in Tagalog and "Dako" in Bisaya. "Small" is "Maliit" in Tagalog and "Gamay" in Bisaya. "Long" is "Mahaba" in Tagalog and "Taas" in Bisaya. "Short" is "Maikli" in Tagalog and "Mubo" in Bisaya.

"Hot" is "Mainit" in Tagalog and "Init" in Bisaya. "Cold" is "Malamig" in Tagalog and "Bugnaw" in Bisaya. "Fast" is "Mabilis" in Tagalog and "Paspas" in Bisaya. "Slow" is "Mabagal" in Tagalog and "Hinay" in Bisaya.

## General Qualities
"Good" is "Mabuti" in Tagalog and "Maayo" in Bisaya. "Bad" is "Masama" in Tagalog and "Dili maayo" in Bisaya. "Easy" is "Madali" in Tagalog and "Sayon" in Bisaya. "Difficult" is "Mahirap" in Tagalog and "Lisod" in Bisaya.

"New" is "Bago" in Tagalog and "Bag-o" in Bisaya. "Old" is "Luma" in Tagalog and "Da-an" in Bisaya. "Clean" is "Malinis" in Tagalog and "Limpyo" in Bisaya. "Dirty" is "Marumi" in Tagalog and "Hugaw" in Bisaya.

"Full" is "Puno" in both languages. "Empty" is "Walang laman" in Tagalog and "Walay sulod" in Bisaya. "Cheap" is "Mura" in Tagalog and "Barato" in Bisaya. "Expensive" is "Mahal" in both languages. "Important" is "Mahalaga" in Tagalog and "Importante" in Bisaya. "Correct" is "Tama" in Tagalog and "Husto" in Bisaya. "Wrong" is "Mali" in Tagalog and "Sayop" in Bisaya.
