# Common Verbs

## Basic Actions
"Eat" is "Kumain" in Tagalog and "Kaon" in Bisaya. "Drink" is "Uminom" in Tagalog and "Inom" in Bisaya. "Sleep" is "Matulog" in Tagalog and "Katulog" in Bisaya. "Wake up" is "Gumising" in Tagalog and "Mata" in Bisaya.

"Go" is "Pumunta" in Tagalog and "Adto" in Bisaya. "Come" is "Pumunta rito" in Tagalog and "Ari" in Bisaya. "Walk" is "Maglakad" in Tagalog and "Lakad" in Bisaya. "Run" is "Tumakbo" in Tagalog and "Dag-an" in Bisaya.

"Sit" is "Umupo" in Tagalog and "Lingkod" in Bisaya. "Stand" is "Tumayo" in Tagalog and "Tindog" in Bisaya. "Read" is "Magbasa" in Tagalog and "Magbasa" in Bisaya. "Write" is "Sumulat" in Tagalog and "Sulat" in Bisaya.

## Communication and Work
"Speak" is "Magsalita" in Tagalog and "Sulti" in Bisaya. "Talk" is "Makipag-usap" in Tagalog and "Makigstorya" in Bisaya. "Listen" is "Makinig" in Tagalog and "Paminaw" in Bisaya. "Learn" is "Matuto" in Tagalog and "Kat-on" in Bisaya. "Teach" is "Magturo" in Tagalog and "Magtudlo" in Bisaya.

"Work" is "Magtrabaho" in Tagalog and "Trabaho" in Bisaya. "Study" is "Mag-aral" in Tagalog and "Magtuon" in Bisaya. "Help" is "Tumulong" in Tagalog and "Tabang" in Bisaya. "Ask" is "Magtanong" in Tagalog and "Pangutana" in Bisaya. "Answer" is "Sumagot" in Tagalog and "Tubag" in Bisaya.
