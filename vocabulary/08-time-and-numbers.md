# Time and Numbers

## Time
"Today" is "Ngayon" in Tagalog and "Karon" in Bisaya. "Tomorrow" is "Bukas" in Tagalog and "Ugma" in Bisaya. "Yesterday" is "Kahapon" in Tagalog and "Gahapon" in Bisaya. "Now" is "Ngayon" in Tagalog and "Karon" in Bisaya. "Later" is "Mamaya" in Tagalog and "Unya" in Bisaya.

"Morning" is "Umaga" in Tagalog and "Buntag" in Bisaya. "Afternoon" is "Hapon" in both languages. "Evening" is "Gabi" in Tagalog and "Gabii" in Bisaya. "Night" is "Gabi" in Tagalog and "Gabii" in Bisaya. "Day" is "Araw" in Tagalog and "Adlaw" in Bisaya.

"Week" is "Linggo" in Tagalog and "Semana" in Bisaya. "Month" is "Buwan" in Tagalog and "Bulan" in Bisaya. "Year" is "Taon" in Tagalog and "Tuig" in Bisaya.

## Numbers
"One" is "Isa" in Tagalog and "Usa" in Bisaya. "Two" is "Dalawa" in Tagalog and "Duha" in Bisaya. "Three" is "Tatlo" in Tagalog and "Tulo" in Bisaya. "Four" is "Apat" in Tagalog and "Upat" in Bisaya. "Five" is "Lima" in both languages.

"Six" is "Anim" in Tagalog and "Unom" in Bisaya. "Seven" is "Pito" in both languages. "Eight" is "Walo" in both languages. "Nine" is "Siyam" in both languages. "Ten" is "Sampu" in Tagalog and "Napulo" in Bisaya.
