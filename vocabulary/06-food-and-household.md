# Food and Household

## Food and Drinks
"Food" is "Pagkain" in Tagalog and "Pagkaon" in Bisaya. "Water" is "Tubig" in both languages. "Rice" is "Kanin" in Tagalog and "Kan-on" in Bisaya. "Bread" is "Tinapay" in Tagalog and "Pan" in Bisaya.

"Meat" is "Karne" in both languages. "Chicken" is "Manok" in both languages. "Pork" is "Baboy" in both languages. "Beef" is "Baka" in both languages. "Fish" is "Isda" in both languages. "Egg" is "Itlog" in both languages.

"Vegetable" is "Gulay" in both languages. "Fruit" is "Prutas" in both languages. "Salt" is "Asin" in both languages. "Sugar" is "Asukal" in both languages. "Coffee" is "Kape" in both languages. "Milk" is "Gatas" in both languages.

## Meals and Household Items
"Breakfast" is "Almusal" in Tagalog and "Pamahaw" in Bisaya. "Lunch" is "Tanghalian" in Tagalog and "Paniudto" in Bisaya. "Dinner" is "Hapunan" in Tagalog and "Panihapon" in Bisaya.

"Kitchen" is "Kusina" in both languages. "House" is "Bahay" in Tagalog and "Balay" in Bisaya. "Room" is "Silid" in Tagalog and "Kwarto" in Bisaya. "Bathroom" is "Banyo" in both languages. "Door" is "Pinto" in Tagalog and "Pultahan" in Bisaya. "Window" is "Bintana" in both languages.
