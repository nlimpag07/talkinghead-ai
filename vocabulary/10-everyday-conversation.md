# Everyday Conversation

## Understanding and Assistance
"I don't know" is "Hindi ko alam" in Tagalog and "Wala ko kahibalo" in Bisaya. "I understand" is "Naiintindihan ko" in Tagalog and "Kasabot ko" in Bisaya. "I don't understand" is "Hindi ko naiintindihan" in Tagalog and "Wala ko kasabot" in Bisaya.

"I need help" is "Kailangan ko ng tulong" in Tagalog and "Kinahanglan nako og tabang" in Bisaya. "Help me" is "Tulungan mo ako" in Tagalog and "Tabangi ko" in Bisaya. "Please wait" is "Mangyaring maghintay" in Tagalog and "Palihog hulat" in Bisaya.

"Please come here" is "Halika rito, pakiusap" in Tagalog and "Palihog ari diri" in Bisaya. "Please sit down" is "Umupo ka, pakiusap" in Tagalog and "Palihog lingkod" in Bisaya. "Speak slowly" is "Magsalita ka nang mabagal" in Tagalog and "Hinay-hinay pagsulti" in Bisaya.

## Preferences and Reassurance
"I am not sure" is "Hindi ako sigurado" in Tagalog and "Dili ko sigurado" in Bisaya. "That's okay" is "Ayos lang iyan" in Tagalog and "Okay ra kana" in Bisaya. "No problem" is "Walang problema" in Tagalog and "Walay problema" in Bisaya. "Don't worry" is "Huwag kang mag-alala" in Tagalog and "Ayaw kabalaka" in Bisaya.

"I like it" is "Gusto ko ito" in Tagalog and "Ganahan ko niini" in Bisaya. "I don't like it" is "Hindi ko ito gusto" in Tagalog and "Dili ko ganahan niini" in Bisaya. "I love you" is "Mahal kita" in Tagalog and "Gihigugma tika" in Bisaya. "I miss you" is "Miss kita" in Tagalog and "Gimingaw ko nimo" in Bisaya.
