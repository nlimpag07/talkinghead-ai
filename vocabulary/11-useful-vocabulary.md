# Useful Everyday Vocabulary

## Money, Work, and Information
"Money" is "Pera" in Tagalog and "Kwarta" in Bisaya. "Price" is "Presyo" in both languages. "Work" is "Trabaho" in both languages. "Business" is "Negosyo" in both languages. "Problem" is "Problema" in both languages.

"Question" is "Tanong" in Tagalog and "Pangutana" in Bisaya. "Answer" is "Sagot" in Tagalog and "Tubag" in Bisaya. "Information" is "Impormasyon" in both languages. "Message" is "Mensahe" in both languages. "Name" is "Pangalan" in Tagalog and "Ngalan" in Bisaya.

## Technology and Media
"Phone" is "Telepono" in both languages. "Computer" is "Kompyuter" in both languages. "Book" is "Libro" in both languages. "Password" is "Password" in both languages. "Internet" is "Internet" in both languages. "Website" is "Website" in both languages. "Email" is "Email" in both languages.

"Photo" is "Larawan" in Tagalog and "Litrato" in Bisaya. "Video" is "Bidyo" in Tagalog and "Video" in Bisaya. "Music" is "Musika" in both languages. "Song" is "Kanta" in Tagalog and "Awit" in Bisaya. "Movie" is "Pelikula" in Tagalog and "Salida" in Bisaya. "News" is "Balita" in both languages.
